Someone in the group says it first: *tuma kwa number yangu*. Then the list starts. Names, amounts, a tick when the M-Pesa message comes in, and one or two people who will send tomorrow.

It mostly works. But it puts one person's number in front of everyone, mixes the group's money with their own, and makes them the one who chases. Nobody else can see the total without asking them.

A Quick Pool replaces the number and the list with one link.

## What a Quick Pool is

A page with a name, a target and a closing date. Anyone with the link can pool into it by M-Pesa: they enter their number and an amount, then approve the prompt on their phone or pay by paybill. They don't need an app or a GrupChat account.

Everyone who opens the link sees the same page: how much has come in, how close the pool is to its target, and when it closes. They see amounts, never names or phone numbers.

## Start one in four steps

You need a GrupChat account with a verified email to start a pool. Then:

1. **Name it.** Everyone with the link sees the name, so call it what you're pooling for: "Kevo's send-off", "Naivasha weekend".
2. **Set a target.** The amount you're aiming for. The progress bar fills towards it.
3. **Pick when it closes.** A day, a few days, a week, a month, or a date you choose. When it closes, pooling stops.
4. **Choose how many people can pool.** Quick Pools are private for now: anyone with the link can pool, up to the number of people you set. You can raise it later.

Then share the link wherever your group already talks.

## How your friends pool

They open the link and tap **Pool**, then enter their M-Pesa number and an amount. From there, they choose:

- **M-Pesa prompt.** A prompt arrives on their phone and they enter their PIN. The pool page updates when M-Pesa confirms.
- **Paybill.** If they'd rather pay from the M-Pesa menu, or the prompt doesn't come through, they get the business number and an account number that is theirs for this pool. They use the same one every time.

Their number is never shown to the group. If they want receipts, they can add an email and get a sign-in link. There's no password.

## When the pool closes

The person who started the pool withdraws the money to M-Pesa. Every payout is checked by a person on our side before it leaves.

If someone needs their money back, they can ask for a refund from the pool page. The person who started the pool approves it, and it goes back to the M-Pesa number it came from.

If the pool is full, people already in it can still add money. A payment from anyone else is returned in full.

## About the "How it's going" note

Each pool has a short note on its progress, written by AI from the pool's totals. It never sees a name, a phone number or an email, only the numbers the whole group can already see.

## What it's good for

Birthdays and gifts. Office send-offs. Weddings. Trips. Team lunches. Anything where a group of people who know each other are chipping in for one thing, by a date.

Quick Pools are for people you know. Open pools, for wider appeals, are not available yet.

## Make it go smoothly

- **Say what the money covers** in the same message as the link: "KES 1,000 each for Kevo's gift and the cake."
- **Post the link once and pin it.** Ask people to pool through the link, not to your number.
- **Remind with the total, not with names.** "We're KES 6,000 away" lands better than chasing one person, and everyone can check it on the page.
